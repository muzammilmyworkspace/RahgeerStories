Why we walk this road

We were born between these mountains.

Rahgeer started with three friends from Gilgit, one jeep and a habit of taking visitors to the places the guidebooks missed. The visitors kept coming back. They brought friends. The friends brought families.

Today we have guided more than two hundred travelers from five countries across Gilgit Baltistan, Kaghan, Swat and now Turkey, Baku, Dubai and Thailand. The jeep count went up. The rule did not change: every trip is personal.

We know which chai stop has the best view of Rakaposhi. We know which road closes after 4pm in September. We know the family in Khaplu who will cook for you like you are their own. That knowledge is the trip.

We are not a booking desk. We are the people who walk with you.
